Ten steps towards AI Act compliance

Step 1: inventory your AI systems. Step 2: classify each system as prohibited, high-risk, limited-risk or minimal-risk. Step 3: for high-risk systems, set up a risk management process and a quality management system. Step 4: prepare the technical documentation described in Annex IV. Step 5: plan human oversight and logging. Step 6: run a conformity assessment. Step 7: register the system. Step 8: monitor it after it is placed on the market. Step 9: train your staff. Step 10: review the list every year.
