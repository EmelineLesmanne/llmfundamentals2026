Key dates for high-risk AI systems

Providers of high-risk AI systems listed in Annex III of the AI Act must comply with the full set of requirements from 2 August 2026. This covers risk management, data governance, technical documentation, record-keeping, transparency, human oversight, accuracy and cybersecurity. Product teams should treat 2 August 2026 as the date from which the AI Act applies to their Annex III systems, and plan the conformity assessment accordingly.

Regulatory sandboxes

Each Member State must have at least one AI regulatory sandbox operational by 2 August 2026. Companies that want to test an Annex III system in a sandbox before that date should contact their national authority now, because places will be limited.

What this means for you

Start your gap analysis early. List every AI system your company places on the market, check whether it falls under Annex III, and assign an owner for each requirement. A realistic plan needs at least six months of preparation before the date above.
