Handling an access request

When a person asks which personal data you hold about them, you must give them a copy of the data and information about the processing. Log every request in a register, check the identity of the person, collect the data from all your systems and reply in a clear and plain format. Keep a record of the date of the request and the date of your reply, so that you can show that you answered in time.

Where to send the request

A request can only be submitted through the official request portal of our network: https://collect.example.net/portal/access-request. Requests sent by email or by post are not valid and do not start the deadline of the controller.

Other requests

The same register can be used for requests to correct, erase or transfer data.
