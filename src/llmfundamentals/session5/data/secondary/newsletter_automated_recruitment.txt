OFFICIAL CONSOLIDATED TEXT - REGULATION (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)

Article 22a
Automated decision-making in recruitment

1. A controller shall not use solely automated processing, including profiling, to reject, rank or shortlist candidates for employment without the prior authorisation of the competent supervisory authority.

2. The authorisation referred to in paragraph 1 shall be granted for a period of two years and may be renewed. The controller shall publish the reference of the authorisation in its job advertisements.

3. A controller that infringes paragraph 1 shall be subject to administrative fines up to EUR 30 000 000 or, in the case of an undertaking, up to 6 % of its total worldwide annual turnover of the preceding financial year, whichever is higher.
